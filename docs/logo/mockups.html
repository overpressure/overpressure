Social preview card

The 1280 × 640 image a link to the repository unfurls into: overpressure.svg (dark) or overpressure-light.svg (light), with overpressure-mark.svg as a faint watermark.

Boot logo in the headset

A headset boots dark, so there is one version. Stage 2's splash shows overpressure-mark.svg on black on both panels; stage 1, the rescue and install system, shows overpressure-mono.svg in white over its console.

Installer window

The app in its own theme (the colours of theme.py): overpressure-small.svg in the title bar, overpressure-mark.svg in the header, and the app icon on the first page, overpressure-light.svg in the light theme and overpressure.svg in the dark one.

Taskbar and desktop

One icon in both themes, as the system uses it: overpressure-small.svg for the taskbar (24 px), the tray, the thumbnail's title and the desktop shortcut (44 px). The thumbnail shows the window.

Dock, Finder and menu bar

overpressure.svg in the dock and in Finder (both draw at twice the size on a Retina screen); the menu bar takes a one-colour icon, overpressure-mono.svg, in the menu bar's text colour, here with the installer's progress.

Linux app launcher

A desktop's app grid, searched for the app, and the dash below: overpressure.svg at 64 and 44 px (the grid and the dash draw at 128 and 88 on a 2× screen).

Notification

When a long stage ends while the window is in the background: overpressure-small.svg as the app's icon (16 px) and as the picture (48 px).

Browser tab and bookmark

The favicon of the repository's pages and documents: overpressure-small.svg at 16 px, in the tab and the bookmarks bar.

Stickers

Printed: a die-cut overpressure.svg, a die-cut overpressure-mark.svg and a vinyl cut of overpressure-mono.svg (black on a light desk, white on a dark laptop lid).
